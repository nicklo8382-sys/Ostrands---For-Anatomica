Modifications:
- 0.8.0.1, by Keevan2374, 2026-10-23:
  - Creatures in SexLab scenes: strands, auto-pairing, collision and semen
    follow each creature's own penis bones, chosen per skeleton from
    [CreatureRigs] in OStrands.ini ([Creatures] Enabled toggles it).
  - Built-in rigs for BakaFactory ABC creature skeletons, based on the ABC
    overrides that ship with PPA - Penetration Physics.
  - Arousal precum for creatures.
- 0.8.0.2, by crajjjj, 2026-10-24:
  - A creature only has a penis while it wears an erect mesh.
  - Creatures without a matching rig get their penis bones from the erect
    mesh they wear.
  - Creatures are kept in OStim scenes too.
  - A rig shaft radius of 0 measures the shaft from the worn mesh.
- 0.8.0.3, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-19:
  - Separate creature semen ([CreatureSemen]: amount, strength, spurts,
    thickness, clumping, stretch, colour) and creature strand thickness
    ([CreatureShape]).
  - Semen colour ([Semen] Colour), drawn with a tinted copy of semen.dds
    written to textures\OStrands\Tint.
  - A receiver without a vagina (men) uses the anus when a scene asks for
    vaginal ([General] NoVaginaUsesAnus).
  - Anatomica link ([Anatomica]): the anus position comes from Anatomica,
    loads that go into an Anatomica anus are handed to Anatomica, and other
    mods can add semen through the exported OStrands_Emit.
- 0.8.0.4, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-20:
  - [CreatureSemen] / [CreatureShape] renamed Colossal Load ([ColossalLoad];
    the old sections are still read and moved on save). With Anatomica 2.6c+
    Anatomica decides who gets one and how big (exported Anatomica_LoadKind).
- 0.8.0.5, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-20:
  - What is handed to Anatomica is counted in normal loads.
- 0.8.0.6, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-20:
  - [Anatomica] Hold / Overflow: a finish inside an Anatomica anus stays in
    while the tip is near; only the overflow spills round the shaft.
  - [Anatomica] OutStickiness / OutViscosity for what Anatomica lets out.
  - A Colossal Load's globs and ropes are scaled by its thickness.
- 0.8.0.7, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-20:
  - Strings between the rim and the tip on pull-out ([Spawn] PullStrings).
  - Glowing semen ([Semen] Glow / GlowCumColour / GlowColour / GlowStrength),
    and per giver from Anatomica (Anatomica_CumGlow); OStrands_EmitGlow.
  - Beaded strings ([Shape] Beads), splats ([Semen] Splats), thick cords for
    a Colossal Load ([ColossalLoad] Cords).
- 0.8.0.8, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-20:
  - Glow set up once per glowing semen mesh; non-glowing semen left as it was.
  - The mouth after oral: swallow share, dribble from the corners, cough,
    nose drip; spit drool strings, spit on the shaft, drool after oral.
  - Swallowing reported to Anatomica (Anatomica_Swallow); precum by OStim
    excitement ([Precum]), into openings (Anatomica_Precum), precum strings.
  - Facials ([Oral] FacialChance, reported with Anatomica_Facial), kissing
    strings and snowballing ([Oral] KissStrings, Snowball), squeeze-out of a
    full opening on deep thrusts and on going in again.
  - OStim Beast Form support ([BeastForm]): beast forms count as people,
    its "Big loads" option read from its script, knot strings, wider beast
    mouths, fur cling, knot hold, tongue drool; Vampire Lords: their own big
    load, drool from the mouth, blood-dark cum, cold thin cum, drips off while
    hovering, strands snap when they burst into bats, red drool, fang strings,
    faster drying in the sun.
  - Stains and splashes (decals via BGSDecalManager), rain / water washing,
    nose overflow at a mouth finish; decal textures in textures\OStrands\decals.
- 0.8.0.9, personal build (Claude for Riotguards), 2026-11-21:
  - Stains: decals alpha-tested and one splat per texture (no more squares
    on the floor).
  - The mouth: drool from one corner at a time and strings down the side of
    the chin; dribbles alternate corners and slide down the chin, let go at
    the jaw; after the scene most of what is left is swallowed.
  - No identifiers named near/far (windows.h macros).
  - [Oral] SuperMessy: more strings round the lips, spit webs between them,
    more on the shaft, longer drool. World stains off by default ([Stains] World).
